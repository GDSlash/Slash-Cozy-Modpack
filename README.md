# 🏕️ Slash's Cozy Modpack

Welcome to **Slash's Cozy Modpack**, a carefully curated Fabric experience for Minecraft 1.21.11!

Designed from the ground up with casual survival and laid-back streaming in mind, this pack takes the vanilla Minecraft experience and wraps it in a warm blanket. Whether you want to build a beautiful cabin in the woods, explore sprawling landscapes at your own pace, or just hang out with friends on stream, this pack provides the perfect, stutter-free foundation.

## ✨ What's Inside?

To make things easy to read, the mod list has been broken down into what they bring to your cozy adventure:

### 🎙️ Multiplayer & Streaming Ready

Playing with friends has never been easier or more immersive.

- **Essential Mod** - Host singleplayer worlds and invite your friends seamlessly without a server!

- **Simple Voice Chat** - Talk to your friends with proximity chat for that extra level of immersion.

- **Chat Heads** - See who you're talking to right in the chat box.

### 🎨 Visuals, UI & Ambience

Bringing the world to life with expressive movements, beautiful menus, and atmospheric lighting.

- **LambDynamicLights** - Hold a torch to light up the dark caverns around you.

- **Not Enough Animations & HMI Pose Editor** - Expressive, lively player animations.

- **Hold My Items** - Visually see what you're carrying.

- **Continuity** - Beautiful, connected textures for blocks like glass.

- **Fancy Menu & Reimagined Intro** - A gorgeous, customized menu experience.

- **Drippy Loading Screen** - Say goodbye to the boring red Mojang screen.

- **Mod Menu** - Easily configure all your installed mods in one place.

### 🎒 Quality of Life & Exploration

Taking the frustration out of survival so you can focus on the fun stuff.

- **Xaero's Minimap & World Map** - Never lose your way home again.

- **Just Enough Items (JEI)** - The ultimate recipe viewer; never alt-tab to a wiki again!

- **Inventory Profiles Next** - Keep your chests and inventory perfectly organized with a single click.

- **AppleSkin** - Take the guesswork out of eating by seeing exactly how much hunger food restores.

- **Shulker Box Tooltip** - Peek inside your shulker boxes directly from your inventory.

- **Accurate Block Placement Reborn** - Build faster and smarter.

- **Zoomify** - A sleek, cinematic zoom for getting a better look at distant landscapes.

### 🚀 Silky Smooth Performance

Nobody likes stream lag. These mods work together to ensure your game runs like butter.

- **Sodium & Iris Shaders** - The ultimate rendering duo for skyrocketing your FPS and enabling shaders.

- **Dynamic FPS** - Lowers resource usage when you alt-tab to read stream chat.

- **Entity Culling & More Culling** - Stops rendering things you can't see to save processing power.

- **FerriteCore** - Drastically reduces the memory (RAM) your game needs.

- **ImmediatelyFast & RenderScale** - Under-the-hood tweaks for lightning-fast rendering.
(Note: CreativeCore is also included as a required library for some of our amazing mods!)

### 🍃 Hand-Picked Resource Packs

To push the cozy vibe even further, this pack comes pre-loaded with a few resource packs that make the world feel lush and the menus easy on the eyes:

- **Motschen's Better Leaves** - Fluffy, bushy leaves that make forests feel alive.

- **Fast Better Grass** - Smooth, seamless grass blocks for rolling hills.

- **Recolourful Containers GUI + HUD (Dark)** - A beautiful, dark-mode user interface that's perfect for late-night gaming.

- **Low On Fire** - Reduces the height of the fire animation so it doesn't block your screen if you accidentally take a tumble into lava.

### 🌅 A Shader for Every Vibe

Whether you're playing on a high-end streaming rig or a trusty older laptop, we've included three fantastic shader packs so you can choose your perfect level of realism:

- **🟢 Mellow** *(Lowest Graphics)* - A lightweight, charming shader that adds lovely lighting without melting your PC.

- **🟡 Complementary Shaders - Unbound** *(Balanced)* - The perfect middle ground. Gorgeous water, fantastic lighting, and great performance.

- **🔴 Bliss Shaders** *(Hyperrealistic)* - For the ultimate screenshot and streaming flex. Volumetric clouds, intense shadows, and breathtaking atmospheres.
